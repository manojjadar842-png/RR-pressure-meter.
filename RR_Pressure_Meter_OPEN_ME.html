How the demonstration model works

  • Reads ball-by-ball, matches and competition CSVs from the supplied ZIP; includes IPL and T20I matches, excludes super overs.
  • Calculates pre-delivery pressure from innings phase, run requirement, wickets remaining and scoring context. A ball is currently marked high-pressure when its pressure index is at least 65/100.
  • Compares each player's performance under pressure with their non-pressure baseline. Batting uses strike rate and dismissals; bowling uses economy, wickets and dot balls.
  • Requires ≥60 pressure balls, ≥100 normal balls and pressure appearances in ≥5 matches. Smaller samples are discounted by a shrinkage factor.
  • Reports a cohort-relative percentile rating. A score of 100 does not mean a 100% probability of success.

This HTML dashboard is fully offline; it displays the rankings computed by the accompanying Python engine. The analysis does not run anew inside the browser. To recompute scores, use the Python project included in the Windows ZIP.
